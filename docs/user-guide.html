User Guide

Complete guide to using MedCare Rural Hospital Management System effectively.

Getting Started

First Login

  1. Open your web browser and navigate to your hospital system URL
  2. Click "Access Hospital System" from the landing page
  3. Enter your login credentials provided by your administrator
  4. Click "Sign In" to access the dashboard
Default Credentials: If you're the first user, use the super admin credentials provided in the installation guide.

Navigation Overview

The system is organized into modules accessible via the top navigation tabs:

  • Dashboard: Overview of hospital operations and key metrics
  • Patients: Patient registration and records management
  • Appointments: Scheduling and appointment management
  • Doctors: Doctor schedules and availability
  • Records: Electronic medical records
  • Lab: Laboratory test management
  • Beds: Bed occupancy and room management
  • Billing: Financial transactions and invoicing
  • Inventory: Medicine and supply management
  • Users: Staff management (admin only)

User Interface Elements

  • Header: Hospital name, user profile, and logout
  • Quick Stats: Real-time overview of key metrics
  • Navigation Tabs: Access to different modules
  • Action Buttons: Primary actions like "Add Patient" or "New Appointment"
  • Filter Controls: Search and filter options

Patient Management

Registering a New Patient

  1. Navigate to the Patients tab
  2. Click "Add New Patient" button
  3. Fill in the required information:
    • Personal details (name, age, gender, contact)
    • Address and emergency contact
    • Medical history and allergies
    • Insurance information
    • Vaccination records
  4. Click "Save Patient" to complete registration
Patient ID: A unique patient ID is automatically generated upon registration.

Searching for Patients

Use the search functionality to quickly find patients:

  • Quick Search: Type patient name, phone, or ID in the search box
  • Advanced Filters: Filter by age, gender, department, or status
  • Recent Patients: View recently added or updated patients

Updating Patient Information

  1. Find and select the patient from the list
  2. Click "Edit" or the patient name to open details
  3. Modify the required information
  4. Click "Update Patient" to save changes

Patient Profile Features

  • Basic Information: Personal and contact details
  • Medical History: Chronic conditions and past treatments
  • Vaccination Records: Immunization history
  • Emergency Contacts: Family members and guardians
  • Insurance Details: Policy information and coverage
  • Appointment History: Past and upcoming appointments
  • Billing History: Payment records and outstanding amounts

Appointment System

Scheduling an Appointment

  1. Go to the Appointments tab
  2. Click "Schedule New Appointment"
  3. Select or search for the patient
  4. Choose the doctor and department
  5. Pick an available date and time slot
  6. Add appointment notes or symptoms
  7. Confirm the appointment

Managing Appointments

Today's Appointments

  • View all scheduled appointments
  • Check-in patients as they arrive
  • Update appointment status
  • Handle no-shows and cancellations

Calendar View

  • Monthly/weekly calendar display
  • Drag-and-drop rescheduling
  • Doctor availability overview
  • Conflict detection

Appointment Status

Status Description Actions Available
Scheduled Appointment booked and confirmed Check-in, Reschedule, Cancel
Checked-in Patient has arrived and waiting Start consultation, Cancel
In Progress Consultation is ongoing Complete, Add notes
Completed Consultation finished View notes, Generate bill
Cancelled Appointment cancelled Reschedule
No-show Patient didn't attend Reschedule, Contact patient

Appointment Reminders

The system automatically sends reminders via:

  • SMS: 24 hours and 2 hours before appointment
  • Email: Appointment confirmations and changes
  • In-app notifications: For staff members

Medical Records

Creating a Medical Record

  1. Navigate to Records tab or start from an appointment
  2. Select the patient
  3. Choose "New Consultation" or "Follow-up"
  4. Fill in the consultation details:
    • Chief complaint and symptoms
    • Vital signs measurements
    • Physical examination findings
    • Diagnosis and treatment plan
    • Prescriptions and recommendations
  5. Save the medical record

Medical Record Components

Consultation Notes

Vital Signs

Diagnosis & Treatment

  • ICD-10 codes

Prescriptions

  • Drug interactions check

Inventory Management

Adding Inventory Items

  1. Navigate to Inventory tab

Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

Issue: Cannot login to the system
Solutions:
  • Check username and password for typos
  • Ensure caps lock is off
  • Contact administrator to reset password
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
Issue: Page loads slowly or times out
Solutions:
  • Check internet connection
  • Refresh the page
  • Clear browser cache
  • Try a different browser
  • Contact IT support if problem persists
Issue: Data not saving properly
Solutions:
  • Ensure all required fields are filled
  • Check for validation errors
  • Try saving again after a few seconds
  • Refresh page and try again
  • Report to administrator if issue continues
